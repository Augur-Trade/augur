# A.U.G.U.R Cards

<p align="center"><img src="../../media/gifs/card.gif" alt="A.U.G.U.R Card" width="380"></p>

Virtual **Visa and Mastercard** cards for holders. Top up with SOL from your wallet, then spend online, in apps and in stores through Google Wallet.

## Who can get one

| You hold | Cards |
|---|---|
| 5M+ tokens | 1 card |
| 10M+ tokens | 3 cards |

## Using a card

- **Top up with SOL** in one tap from any of your wallets
- **Freeze, reload or close** a card at any time
- Add it to **Google Wallet** to tap and pay in stores
- Choose from **six card designs**

Open **Wallet → Card**, or **More → A.U.G.U.R Card**.

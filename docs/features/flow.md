# Flow

Live market flow on Solana: **the biggest buys as they happen**, hourly volume, and the coins wallets are piling into.

Open it from **More → FOMO**, or the market strip on Home.

## What you see

- **Live tape:** large buys streaming in, each with the token, the size in SOL and USD, and the buyer
- **Hot coins:** where the most buying is happening right now
- **Hourly volume:** how much is trading right now

## Acting on it

- Tap a token to open its page, with the chart and risk scan.
- Tap a buyer to see the wallet, then **Copy trades** to set up [copy trading](copy-trading.md) with the backtest preview already loaded.

> [!TIP]
> Big buys aren't always smart buys. Developers, insiders and bots buy big too. Check the risk scan and the wallet's copy grade before following anything you see on the tape.

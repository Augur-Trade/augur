# Holder rewards

**Half of all A.U.G.U.R platform fees go to token holders**, paid **daily in SOL**, straight to your wallet. There's nothing to stake and nothing to claim.

## Tiers

| You hold | You share in |
|---|---|
| **5M+** tokens | A pool of **12.5%** of fees, split by balance |
| **10M+** tokens | The 12.5% pool **plus** a second pool of **37.5%**, split by balance |

## Payouts

- Daily at **12:00 UTC**
- Paid in **SOL** to the wallet holding the tokens

Live totals (SOL paid out, wallets earning, the next payout) are on [augur-trade.com](https://augur-trade.com).

> [!WARNING]
> Rewards are sent automatically. There is **never** a claim page, a migration or an airdrop to sign for. Any site asking you to connect a wallet to "claim" A.U.G.U.R rewards is a scam.

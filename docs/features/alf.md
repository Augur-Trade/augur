# A.L.F

<p align="center"><img src="../../media/gifs/augur-blink.gif" alt="A.L.F" width="140"></p>

**A.L.F** (*Autonomous Learning Framework*) is the agent inside A.U.G.U.R.

## Token reads

Paste any contract address and get a plain-language read in seconds, built from live on-chain data:

1. **Liquidity and pool health:** depth, locks and how easily you can exit
2. **Buy pressure:** who is buying, who is selling and how fast
3. **Holder flow:** concentration, fresh wallets and insiders
4. **Socials:** whether there's a real community behind it

Every read ends with one verdict, **BUY**, **WATCH** or **AVOID**, and the reasoning behind it.

## Briefings

On Home, A.L.F briefs you on your open bags in one line ("MOODENG leads at +34%, Fartcoin is your weakest at −7.6%"). Tap **Ask** to follow up.

## Ask anything

Ask what's moving, how a token is set up, or what a term in the app means. Try it free on [augur-trade.com/alf](https://augur-trade.com/alf/): three messages without an account.

> [!WARNING]
> A.L.F can be wrong. Its reads are based on data at that moment, and memecoins change in seconds. It never trades for you. Your settings do.

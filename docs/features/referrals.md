# Referrals

Invite friends and earn a share of the fees they pay.

Open **More → Invite**.

## What you earn

- **Up to 25% of your invitees' fees.** Your share starts at 10% and climbs through the tiers shown on the Invite screen as you bring in more active traders.
- **A second level:** a further share (3%) of fees from people *your* invitees bring in.

## What your friends get

**10% of their fees back for their first 14 days.**

## Your invite tools

- your link and QR code
- one-tap share to Telegram and X
- a **vanity code** (your own short name)
- a flex card image to post
- your crew (who joined through you) and the leaderboard

## Rules

- Invites only count for **new users**, people who haven't paid fees before.
- No self-invites, and no invite loops.
- Accounts linked on chain to the inviter (funded from the same wallet, for example) are flagged and don't earn.

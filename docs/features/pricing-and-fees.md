# Pricing and fees

| | |
|---|---|
| **Buys** | 2% |
| **Sells** | Free |
| **Subscription** | None. Every feature is included |
| **Risk scan** | Free on every token |
| **Whale copy** | 10% of the profit on winning copies only |
| **Referrals** | Invitees get 10% of their fees back for 14 days |

## Buys

A 2% fee is taken on every buy: manual, sniped, from calls, copy trading or Top Picks. Selling costs nothing on top of the network fee.

## Whale copy

[Whale copy](whale-copy.md) has no buy fee. Instead:
- **10% of the profit** on each copy that closes in profit, sent from your wallet when it closes
- **nothing** on losing or break-even copies
- no fee unless the profit was confirmed from the copy's own transactions

## Network costs (not A.U.G.U.R fees)

These go to the Solana network, not to us:

- **Transaction fee + priority fee**: A fraction of a cent to a few cents, higher when the network is busy. Comes back: no.
- **Token-account rent**: About 0.002 SOL per new token you buy. Comes back: yes, when the account is closed ([reclaim rent](wallets.md#rent-and-reclaiming-it)).
- **Whale-copy position rent**: About 0.06 SOL per open copy. Comes back: yes, when the copy closes.

## Where the fees go

**Half of all platform fees go to token holders**, paid daily in SOL. See [Holder rewards](holder-rewards.md).

# App tour

The same app runs in the browser, in Telegram and on Android.

<p align="center"><img src="../../media/screens.jpg" alt="Home, Calls, Snipe and Wallet" width="100%"></p>

## Header

- **SOL | EVM** switches the whole app between Solana and the [13 EVM chains](../features/multichain.md).
- **Chat** opens the community room.
- **⊞ More** opens every other tool (see below).

## Bottom bar

### Home
Your desk at a glance.
- **Portfolio value** across every wallet, today's change, SOL balance, unrealised P&L and open positions.
- **Quick actions:** Scan a token, Pay, Receive and Buy SOL.
- **A.L.F briefing:** a one-line read on your open bags, with an **Ask** button. See [A.L.F](../features/alf.md).
- **Whale copy** shortcut. See [Whale copy](../features/whale-copy.md).
- **Live positions:** each one shows entry → now, P&L in % and SOL, its age, and which wallet holds it. Tap one for the chart and sell controls.
- **Recent trades:** every buy, sell, snipe and take-profit across your wallets, including on-chain swaps you made outside the app.
- **Top picks and market flow:** what's moving right now.

### Calls
Every contract address posted in the channels you follow, scored on what it did after the call. Filter, sort, and optionally auto-buy what passes. See [Calls and Top Picks](../features/calls-and-top-picks.md).

### Buy ⚡
The centre button. Paste a contract address, read the scan, pick an amount and wallets, check your exits and buy. The chart sits on top with buy and sell right under it. See [Buying and selling](../features/buying-and-selling.md).

### Snipe
Your channel and X snipers: armed or paused, each with its own buy size and exits. Also **Snipe Top Picks** and a list of recent snipes. See [Channel sniper](../features/channel-sniper.md).

### Wallet
Every wallet and every chain in one balance: holdings, send and receive, move SOL between wallets, reclaim rent, export keys. Also your [A.U.G.U.R Card](../features/cards.md). See [Wallets](../features/wallets.md).

## Token page

Tap any token anywhere in the app to open its page:
- a live chart with one shared price, so the chart, the card and the header always agree
- market cap, liquidity, holders, age and socials (one X link per token)
- the risk scan and bundle check
- your position in it, with **Add to position**, **Sell** and **Remove position, keep tokens**

## Little things worth knowing

- **(i) icons** explain any label. Tap one for a plain-English definition.
- **Token age chips** show how old every coin is, everywhere it appears.
- **Share cards:** turn a win, an open bag or a daily recap into an image to post.

# Changelog

Notable releases, newest first.

## October 2026

- **Snipers switch off on an empty wallet** (20 Oct): two calls in a row without the SOL to buy and your snipers pause, with a message to top up. [Docs](../features/channel-sniper.md#speed-and-safety)
- **Faster channel sniper** (20 Oct): calls are picked up about a second after they're posted, and the buy lands about half a second later.
- **Official group** (19 Oct): [t.me/AUGUR_Trade](https://t.me/AUGUR_Trade), hosted by A.L.F. Launch news lands there first.
- **No double buys** (18 Oct): a timed-out fast route is checked against your wallet before any other route buys. [Docs](../features/channel-sniper.md#speed-and-safety)
- **MAD → A.U.G.U.R 1:1** (17 Oct): $MAD holders carry over 1:1 through [augur-trade.com/collect](https://augur-trade.com/collect) until 24 Oct, 08:23 UTC.
- **Liquidity on every launchpad** (17 Oct): risk scans read a bonding curve's real liquidity from the chain for any launchpad, not just pump.fun, and pick the curve over tiny side pools. [Docs](../features/risk-scan.md)
- **Invite links on augur-trade.com** (17 Oct): invite links, QR codes, share text and invite passes all point to the A.U.G.U.R invite page. [Docs](../features/referrals.md)
- **Whale copy** (17 Oct): copy Meteora DLMM liquidity whales from your bot wallet.
  - Discover views: *Earning now*, *All*, *A.U.G.U.R* (our own engine's whales and finds) and *New finds*.
  - Ranked by measured copy results, never by the whale's own profit.
  - Whale books show SOL pairs only, split into SOL net and tokens out.
  - 10% of profit on winning copies, nothing on losses.
  - [Docs](../features/whale-copy.md)
- **A.U.G.U.R** (16 Oct): new name, new look, new site at [augur-trade.com](https://augur-trade.com), self-hosted fonts and a simpler buy flow.
- **Live prices everywhere** (15 Oct): charts, cards and positions all marked to the same live price.
- **Snipe Top Picks** (13 Oct): auto-buy coins the pick model scores 90+. Top Picks rebuilt on a trained model.
- **Widgets** (11 Oct): P&L, positions and whale stats on your Android home screen or as a floating overlay.
- **Rugscope v3** (10 Oct): ten engines and a dump-probability model. [Augur-Trade/rugscope](https://github.com/Augur-Trade/rugscope)
- **Rug guard for snipers** (9 Oct): serial developers, concentrated supply and bundled launches are skipped before buying.
- **Complete trade history** (9 Oct): Recent trades now includes on-chain swaps made outside the app.
- **Token age** on every coin (9 Oct).
- **Crews and profiles** (3–6 Oct): crew chats, call tracking and public profiles.
- **Multichain** (4 Oct): 13 EVM chains and a bridge between any of them and Solana.
- **Non-SOL pairs** (5 Oct): tokens that trade against other coins route through a swap aggregator automatically.
- **Connect wallet** (2 Oct): browse read-only with your Phantom or Solflare balances.
- **Referrals** (1 Oct): up to 25% of invitees' fees, plus 10% cashback for invitees.
- **Scan guard on Calls** (1 Oct): every call scanned, with safety rings and filters to skip Avoid tokens.

# Glossary

**A.L.F.** Autonomous Learning Framework, the AI agent inside A.U.G.U.R that reads tokens and briefs you on your bags. See [A.L.F](../features/alf.md).

**A.U.G.U.R.** Autonomous Unit for Gauging Upside & Risk.

**Bin (DLMM).** A narrow price slot in a Meteora DLMM pool. Liquidity sits in bins, and earns fees when the price trades through them.

**Bonding curve.** How a new pump.fun token is priced before it has a real pool. "On the curve" means it hasn't graduated yet.

**Bot wallet.** A Solana wallet A.U.G.U.R signs with so trades land instantly. It's yours, and you can export its key at any time.

**Bundle.** Several wallets buying in the same block as a launch, often funded from one source. A common setup for a coordinated dump.

**Call.** A contract address posted in a channel you follow.

**Contract address (CA).** A token's unique address on chain. Pasting it is how you find and buy a token.

**Copy-backtest.** A replay of what copying a wallet would have returned, entering seconds after it like a real copy does, with fees.

**Deep loss.** A copy that lost a large share of what went in.

**DLMM.** Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker, Meteora's pool type with liquidity placed in price bins.

**Exits.** Take-profit, stop-loss, tiers and trailing stops that sell a position automatically. See [Automated exits](../features/automated-exits.md).

**Fades (copy grade).** A wallet that makes money itself, but whose copiers, arriving seconds later, don't.

**Flat (copy result).** A copy that closed at roughly break-even.

**Freeze authority.** A power to freeze holders' tokens. If it isn't revoked, you might not be able to sell.

**Graduated.** A pump.fun token that has completed its bonding curve and moved to a real pool.

**Honeypot.** A token you can buy but not sell.

**Impermanent loss.** What a liquidity provider loses when the price moves: the pool sells the rising asset and buys the falling one along the way, so you exit holding more of whatever fell.

**Lot.** One wallet's holding of one token. Buying a token from three wallets makes three lots.

**Mint authority.** A power to create more tokens. If it isn't revoked, supply can be inflated.

**Money maker (whale copy).** A whale copiers are profiting on right now. See [the criteria](../features/whale-copy.md#earning-now-criteria).

**Per copy.** The average return of one copy, as a % of what went in.

**Priority fee.** An optional tip that helps your transaction land faster when the network is busy.

**Rent.** A refundable SOL deposit Solana holds on every account (about 0.002 SOL per token account). It comes back when the account is closed.

**Rug.** A token whose developer or insiders pull liquidity or dump supply, crashing the price.

**Serial launcher.** A developer who launches token after token, usually selling each one quickly.

**Slippage.** The most the price is allowed to move against you between sending a trade and it landing.

**SOL net (whale book).** The SOL a whale actually got back minus what it put in, across SOL pairs.

**Stop-loss (SL).** An exit that sells when a position is down by your limit.

**Take-profit (TP).** An exit that sells when a position is up by your target.

**Tiered take-profit.** Selling part of a position at each of several profit levels.

**Token-2022.** A newer Solana token standard that can include transfer fees, transfer hooks, freezing and more. The risk scan flags these.

**Tokens out (whale book).** Tokens a whale withdrew, valued at the price when withdrawn. Only real if it sold them then.

**Top Picks.** Coins scored 0–100 by A.U.G.U.R's pick model. See [Calls and Top Picks](../features/calls-and-top-picks.md#top-picks).

**Trailing stop.** A stop that follows the price up and sells when it falls back by a set distance from the high.

**Whale.** In whale copy, a wallet providing liquidity on Meteora DLMM pools at size.

**Win rate.** The share of trades or copies that closed in profit.
